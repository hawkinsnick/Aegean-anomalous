# Linear A method parity and critical frontier

This project is an evidence quarantine, not a script-invention project. Its two included cases are the Arkalochori Axe and Malia Altar Stone; Phaistos is cross-reference-only and ordinary Cretan Hieroglyphic objects remain in their home corpus.

Arkalochori preserves official museum metadata, Flouda's conservation/materiality observations and disputed script/writing classification without canonical assignment. Malia preserves the primary Chapouthier lineage, CHIC/INSCRIBE identifiers and the historically meaningful 14/15/16 segmentation states rather than majority-voting a count.

Remaining material work is rights/access bound: authoritative reusable sign geometry for Arkalochori and direct CHIC/drawing evidence for Malia. Specialist adjudication of writing status, script membership, sign identity and proposed readings remains downstream. No decipherment proposal supplies canonical values.
